TechVenture Ltd.
Annual Report FY2024

Company Overview
TechVenture Ltd. is a leading provider of digital transformation solutions, specializing in software engineering and IT consulting services. Founded in 2010, the company helps global enterprises modernize their technology infrastructure and build scalable software products. Our mission is to accelerate digital innovation for businesses worldwide.

Financial Highlights
The fiscal year 2024 was a period of strong financial performance for TechVenture Ltd. We achieved a total revenue of ₹1,250 crore, representing a robust growth of 18% year-over-year (YoY) compared to ₹1,059 crore in FY2023. 

Our profitability metrics also showed significant improvement. Operating Profit for the year stood at ₹187 crore, translating to a healthy operating margin of 15%. Net Income reached ₹142 crore, up from ₹120 crore in the previous year. Furthermore, the company generated an EBITDA of ₹210 crore, showcasing our operational efficiency. Earnings Per Share (EPS) for FY2024 was reported at ₹14.20.

Segment Breakdown
TechVenture's revenue is primarily derived from two major segments:
- Software Development: This segment continued to be our largest revenue driver, contributing ₹800 crore to the total revenue. Growth in this segment was fueled by increased demand for cloud migration and custom application development.
- IT Services & Consulting: This segment generated ₹450 crore in revenue, reflecting steady demand for strategic IT advisory and managed services.

Management Commentary
"FY2024 has been a milestone year for TechVenture," said the CEO. "Our double-digit revenue growth is a testament to our resilient business model and the relentless dedication of our global teams. We remain committed to expanding our capabilities in AI and cloud computing to capture future growth opportunities."

Balance Sheet and Liquidity
The company maintains a strong balance sheet. As of the end of FY2024, our total long-term debt was well-managed at ₹95 crore. We have a solid liquidity position with cash and cash equivalents totaling ₹320 crore, providing ample flexibility for future investments and capital returns.

Capital Expenditure
During the year, we invested ₹85 crore in capital expenditure (CapEx). These investments were primarily directed towards upgrading our technology infrastructure, expanding our development centers, and enhancing cybersecurity measures.

Risk Factors
Like all businesses, TechVenture operates in a dynamic environment subject to various risks:
1. Regulatory Risks: Changes in data privacy laws and international trade regulations could impact our operations across different geographies.
2. Competition: The IT services industry is highly competitive, and we face constant pressure from both established global players and niche technology firms.
3. Talent Retention: The demand for highly skilled software engineers remains intense. Failure to attract and retain top talent could adversely affect our ability to deliver on client projects and maintain growth.
